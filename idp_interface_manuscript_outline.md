* An interesting question arises here not answered in our previous work: in these neutral sequences, which are capable of generating their own electrostatic potential, what would happen if one were to introduce a symmetric pair of ions. * When we repeat these calculations with the addition of ions, we see that an interfacial electrostatic potential is still present, but it decays slowly as it reaches either bulk, much like a double layer. * And with this, we have demonstrated the minimal requirements for a polyampholyte to produce an electrostatic potential at an interface. There are many interesting implications to these results, such as what would occur when additional non-idealities are introduced (chi interactions and hydrogen bonding), as well as when these results are extended to the dynamics of biocondensate formation where, although the condensates may be neutral, the net charge at the interface may provide a repulsive barrier which inhibit coarsening of the droplets into larger one.
